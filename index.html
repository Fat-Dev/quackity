02 · The Problem

Nobody has measured where the noise actually lives.

Noise from aircraft activity, student movement, and the surrounding environment may be affecting concentration, comfort, and learning performance across campus — but there's currently no objective way to see where it's worst, or whether a fix would actually work.

QUACKity builds a noise monitoring and mapping system that measures sound intensity in the areas that matter most: classrooms and Boys' Dormitory 2. A portable sound meter collects real-time decibel readings, which are processed into a digital noise map of the school. From there, the project studies how noise level relates to student focus, and tests sound-absorbing materials to find a practical, affordable fix.

The result isn't just a map — it's a recommendation the school can actually build: which rooms need treatment first, and what material gets the best acoustic return per rupiah.
